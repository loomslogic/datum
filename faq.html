FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Getting started

How do I get a card?

Contact DATUM using the Get your card button. A moderator will set up your account and card, and you can then edit it yourself anytime.

Do the people I share it with need an account or app?

No. Your card is a normal web page, so anyone can open it in their phone browser.

Does it work with NFC cards and QR codes?

Yes. Tap an NFC card or scan your QR code and your card opens in the phone browser. Most modern phones can read NFC.

What if a phone cannot read NFC?

Share your card link or QR code instead. The same card opens either way.

Your card

Who can edit my card?

Only you, after logging in. Accounts are created by DATUM moderators, and visitors can only view a card.

Can I change my User ID later?

Yes, in Account settings. Your permanent NFC link never changes, so your card keeps working. Only the older name-based link changes.

Can I hide my phone number and email?

Yes. By default the card only shows Call, Text and Email buttons. You can choose to show the actual details in the dashboard.

What can I put on my card?

Your photo and cover picture, bio, contact buttons, social media and other links, experience, education, skills, a photo gallery and e-wallet QR codes such as GCash.

Do my changes show right away?

Yes. Save in the dashboard and your card is updated for everyone who opens it. Nothing needs to be reprinted.

Privacy and safety

Is my information safe?

Your card only shows what you choose to put on it. Only you can edit it after logging in, and passwords are never visible to anyone, including moderators.

What if I forget my password?

Use Forgot password on the login page, or ask a DATUM moderator to reset it for you.

Still have a question?

Message us and we will get back to you.
