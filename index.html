FAQ

Good to know.

How do I get a card?

Contact DATUM. A moderator will set up your account and card, and you can then edit it yourself anytime.

Do the people I share it with need an account or app?

No. Your card is a normal web page, so anyone can open it in their phone browser.

Who can edit my card?

Only you, after logging in. Accounts are created by DATUM moderators, and visitors can only view a card.

Can I change my User ID later?

Yes, in Account settings. Your permanent NFC link never changes, so your card keeps working. Only the older name-based link changes.

Can I hide my phone number and email?

Yes. By default the card only shows "Tap to call" and "Send email" buttons. You can choose to show the actual details in the dashboard.

Does it work with NFC cards and QR codes?

Yes. Tap an NFC card or scan your QR code and your card opens in the phone browser. Most modern phones can read NFC.
